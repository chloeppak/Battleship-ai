      How to play

      1. Your ships are placed for you. Click Randomize my ships to shuffle them.
      2. Click Start game when you are happy with your layout.
      3. Click a square on the Computer's waters board to fire. The computer fires back after each shot.
      4. Sink all five enemy ships before the computer sinks yours. Click New game to start over at any time.
