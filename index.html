      How to play

      1. Your five boats are docked on the Chattahoochee for you. Click Shuffle my boats to move them.
      2. Click Start game when you are happy with your layout.
      3. Click a square on the Rival's waters board to fire. The rival crew fires back after each shot.
      4. Sink all five rival boats, from the Peachtree to the Decatur, before they sink yours. Click New game to start over at any time.
